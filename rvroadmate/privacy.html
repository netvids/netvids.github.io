Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026 | Effective date: April 2, 2026

SEO Medias ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AI Trip Planner RV mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you consent to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide

  • Vehicle Profile Data: Vehicle type, year, make, model, nickname, and tow vehicle details you enter during setup.
  • Chat Messages: Text you send to the AI assistant for trip planning and travel advice.
  • Saved Trips: Locations and routes you choose to save within the App.

b) Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect your device's approximate location to provide relevant route suggestions and nearby recommendations. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and app version for diagnostics and compatibility.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous interaction data such as feature usage frequency, session duration, and crash reports to improve the App.
  • Advertising Data: If you use the free tier, third-party advertising partners may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP address, and general interaction data to serve relevant ads (see Section 4).

c) Purchase Information

When you subscribe to a premium plan, payment processing is handled entirely by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card number, bank account, or other financial details. We receive only a transaction confirmation and subscription status from our payment processor, RevenueCat.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and personalize the AI travel assistant based on your vehicle specifications
  • Calculate accurate route distances, fuel cost estimates, and travel times
  • Recommend campgrounds, stops, and points of interest suited to your vehicle
  • Process and manage your subscription
  • Send you important service updates and announcements
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends to improve App functionality
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats
  • Display relevant advertisements to free-tier users
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: Your vehicle profiles, chat histories, and saved trips are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this locally stored data.
  • Cloud Processing: Chat messages are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS connections to Google's Gemini AI service for processing. Messages are not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Security Measures: We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL) for all data in transit. Local data is stored using the platform's secure storage mechanisms.
  • Data Retention: We retain anonymous analytics data for up to 24 months. Chat messages are processed in real-time and not stored on our servers beyond the active session.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Our App integrates the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini AI (Google LLC): Powers the AI assistant. Chat messages are sent to Google's servers for processing. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Serves advertisements to free-tier users. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to personalize ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's ad settings.
  • Google Maps (Google LLC): Provides location links and map functionality. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc.): Manages subscriptions and in-app purchases. Receives transaction data from app stores. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo / EAS (Expo Inc.): Used for app updates and distribution. May collect basic device and crash data.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Advertising and Your Choices

Free-tier users will see advertisements served by Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized based on your interests, app usage, and device information.

You can manage your ad preferences:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising > Turn off Personalized Ads

You may also upgrade to a Premium subscription to remove all advertisements.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: Delete all your local data by removing vehicles from the App or uninstalling it. For server-side data deletion requests, contact us at privacy@seomedias.ca.
  • Location: Revoke location permissions at any time in your device settings.
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.
  • Correction: Update your vehicle information at any time within the App.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

For users in California, you have additional rights under CCPA, including the right to know what data is collected and the right to request deletion.

Canadian users have rights under PIPEDA, including the right to access and challenge the accuracy of their personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under the applicable age, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@seomedias.ca.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States and Canada, where data protection laws may differ. By using the App, you consent to such transfers. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy within the App and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
